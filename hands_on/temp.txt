Seismological Data Analysis - Practical File
--------------------------------------------
Line 1: Earthquakes generate seismic waves.
Line 2: P-waves are primary waves and travel fastest.
Line 3: S-waves are secondary waves and cannot travel through liquids.
Line 4: Surface waves cause most structural damage.
Line 5: Seismometers record ground motion.
Line 6: Waveform data is often stored in miniSEED format.
Line 7: SAC is widely used for waveform processing.
Line 8: GMT is used for plotting geophysical data.
Line 9: Linux is preferred for scientific computing.
Line 10: Bash scripting helps automate data analysis.
