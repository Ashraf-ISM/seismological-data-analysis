eventStation

Subsetters that depend on both event and station, such as distance, go here. In addition to the ones directly listed here, any origin, network or station subsetter may also be used here within a logical subsetter.

Example 

<eventStationOR>
    <originDepthRange>
        <min>100</min>
        <unit>KILOMETER</unit>
    </originDepthRange>
    <phaseExists>
        <phaseName>P</phaseName>
    </phaseExists>
</eventStationOR>

<midPoint>
