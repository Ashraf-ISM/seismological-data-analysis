properties

The properties contained here tell SOD how to go about its business whereas the items in each of the arms tell SOD what its business is. Go to the page for any property to find out more about it.

Example 

<properties>
  <runName>II and IU 1992-1993</runName>
  <hibernateConfig>tables.hbm.xml</hibernateConfig>
  <proxyHost>myproxy.example.com</proxyHost>
  <proxyScheme>https</proxyScheme>
</properties>
