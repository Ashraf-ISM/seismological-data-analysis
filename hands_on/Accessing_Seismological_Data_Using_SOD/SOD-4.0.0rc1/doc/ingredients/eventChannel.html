eventChannel

Subsetters that depend on both event and channel go here. In addition to the ones directly listed here, any eventStation, origin, network, station or channel subsetter may also be used here within a logical subsetter.

Example 

<eventChannelOR>
    <originDepthRange>
        <min>100</min>
        <unit>KILOMETER</unit>
    </originDepthRange>
    <eventChannelOR>
        <bandCode>B</bandCode>
        <eventChannelAND>
            <bandCode>S</bandCode>
            <distanceRange>
                <unit>DEGREE</unit>
                <min>0</min>
                <max>20</max>
            </distanceRange>
        </eventChannelAND>
    </eventChannelOR>
</eventChannelOR>
